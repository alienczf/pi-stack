# A and B side by side

## 1 Core difference

A keeps obligations and retained evidence in the owned stream repo. Its checked `STATE.md` is the current authority. B keeps obligations, evidence references, and operation outcomes in Prime's service, with retained bytes in runtime storage. Its `STATE.md` is a generated view. A therefore needs file publication and receiving guards to agree. B needs fact commits, worker delivery, and views to agree. Neither proves semantic correctness or is selected under Z17 and Z19. [A §1][A1] [A §5][A5] [B §1][B1]

## 2 Side-by-side per component

A means `31-design-a-detailed.md`. B means `41-design-b-prime-detailed.md`. Feasibility means `40-b-over-prime.md`. References identify sections and component, hook, prompt, or commit IDs. All behavior below is proposed unless explicitly identified as observed. The comparison does not carry forward the earlier recommendation in `30-research-designs.md`.
